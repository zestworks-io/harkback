Fine-tuning on a laptop

We wanted to adapt a seven-billion-parameter model to our support tickets without renting a cluster. This post walks through the setup, what it cost, and what we would do differently next time.

Here is the whole training configuration:

from peft import get_peft_model
# CodeOnlyTerm: rank of the LoRA update
config = make_config(r=8, alpha=16)
model = get_peft_model(model, config)

The LoRA adapters add only a few million trainable parameters, so the optimizer state fits next to the frozen weights on one card. Training took about three hours.

Merging the adapters back into the weights afterwards means serving costs nothing extra.
