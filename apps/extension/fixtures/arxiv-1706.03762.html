Attention Is All You Need

Abstract

We propose a new network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms. It dispenses with recurrence entirely.

2 Notation

Let dk denote the dimension of the queries and keys. The model also uses dk in several places.

3 Scaled Dot-Product Attention

We compute the dot products of the query with all keys, divide each by dk, and apply a softmax function to obtain the weights on the values. The rank r is small.

Attention(Q,K,V)=softmax(QKTdk)V (1)

where Q, K and V are the matrices of queries, keys and values.
