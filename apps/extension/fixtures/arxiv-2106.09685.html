LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models

Abstract

We propose Low-Rank Adaptation, or LoRA, which freezes the pre-trained model weights and injects trainable rank decomposition matrices into each layer of the Transformer architecture. It greatly reduces the number of trainable parameters.

Fine-tuning large models is expensive. We apply LoRA to the attention weights of the Transformer.
